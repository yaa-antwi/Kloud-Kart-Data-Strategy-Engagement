# Kloud Kart: 12-Month Data Strategy Roadmap

| Quarter | Main priority | Work to be completed | Verifiable business outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1: Address immediate risks** | Customer-data protection and website performance | Investigate the Data Protection Commission complaint and submit a response within the 90-day deadline. Identify known locations of customer information, assign departmental owners and establish procedures for access and deletion. Hire the Data Engineer, provision and test the MySQL read replica using the approved second server, and move reporting exports to it only after testing confirms it is suitable. | **1.** The Data Protection Commission response is submitted within the 90-day deadline, with a written record of where the customer's data was held and what action was taken at each location. **2.** By the end of Q1, Monday reporting exports run from the replica rather than the live database. The developer records before-and-after website response times during the reporting period to show whether performance has improved. **3.** The business definitions that Nana and Finance currently use are documented, and any disagreements or unanswered questions are recorded for resolution in Q2. |
| **Q2: Establish reliable reporting** | Consistent business figures | Hire the Analytics Engineer. Work with Nana and Finance to resolve differences in how revenue is calculated. Agree on the definitions and reporting periods, then use SQL to create scheduled reports from the MySQL read replica. Replace the separate manual spreadsheet calculations with shared reports. | **1.** Nana and Finance approve the same documented definitions for key business figures. **2.** Both receive the same Monday figures using those definitions and reporting periods. **3.** Nana no longer needs to rebuild the Monday reports manually from large CSV exports. |
| **Q3: Improve confidence in the data** | Reporting quality and customer-data controls | Introduce checks for missing, duplicated or inconsistent reporting data. Monitor scheduled reports so failures are identified and corrected. Review access to customer information and test whether deletion procedures cover the known data locations. | **1.** Scheduled reports are checked for missing, duplicated or inconsistent data, and identified problems are recorded and assigned for correction. **2.** Reports show when they were last updated, and failed updates are identified and followed up. **3.** Management has documented evidence that access and deletion procedures have been reviewed and tested across the known customer-data locations. |
| **Q4: Prepare for future growth** | Review and plan the next investment | Review reporting reliability and operating costs. Assess whether the current arrangement can support Nigeria's orders, inventory and reporting requirements. Consider forecasting or more frequent reporting only where a clear business need has been identified. | **1.** Management receives a review of reporting reliability and the costs of maintaining the current arrangement. **2.** Management has an assessment of whether the current arrangement can support Nigeria's expected orders, inventory and reporting requirements. **3.** Any proposed future investment, such as forecasting or more frequent reporting, is supported by an identified business need and a clear reason for the additional cost. |
