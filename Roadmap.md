# Kloud Kart: 12-Month Data Strategy Roadmap

| Quarter | Main priority | Work to be completed | Verifiable business outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1: Address immediate risks** | Customer-data protection and website performance | Investigate the Data Protection Commission complaint and submit a response within the 90-day deadline. Identify known locations of customer information, assign departmental owners and create a register of known copies. Review access to the approximately 300,000-person Marketing list, restrict access to authorised staff and record new exports. Document procedures for access, retention and deletion requests. Hire the Data Engineer, provision and test the MySQL read replica using the approved second server, and move reporting exports only after testing confirms it is suitable. | **1.** The Data Protection Commission response is submitted within the 90-day deadline, supported by a written record of locations checked, actions taken and any outstanding retention issues. **2.** Known customer-data locations have named owners, and Marketing's customer list is restricted to authorised users. New exports have an identified purpose, owner and recorded destination. **3.** Monday reporting exports run from the tested replica rather than the live database. The developer records before-and-after website response times during reporting periods. **4.** Nana and Finance's existing business definitions and disagreements are documented for resolution in Q2. |
| **Q2: Establish reliable reporting** | Consistent business figures | Hire the Analytics Engineer. Work with Nana and Finance to identify why their figures differ. Agree on definitions and reporting periods for key figures such as revenue and order count. Use SQL to prepare shared reports from the read replica and schedule daily updates. Replace separate manual calculations only after the shared figures have been checked and approved. | **1.** Nana and Finance approve the same documented definitions and reporting periods for the agreed business figures. **2.** Both receive the same Monday figures from the shared reporting process. **3.** Nana no longer needs to rebuild the Monday reports manually from large CSV exports. **4.** Reports show their last update time, and a named person is responsible for investigating failed updates. |
| **Q3: Improve confidence in the data** | Reporting quality and customer-data controls | Introduce checks for missing, duplicated or inconsistent reporting data. Monitor scheduled reports so failures are identified and corrected. Review customer-data access and test whether deletion procedures cover the known data locations. Check that backup retention and restoration procedures do not silently reintroduce information that should remain deleted. | **1.** Scheduled reports are checked for missing, duplicated or inconsistent data, and identified problems are recorded and assigned for correction. **2.** Reports show when they were last updated, and failed updates are identified and followed up. **3.** Management has documented evidence that access and deletion procedures have been reviewed and tested across known customer-data locations. **4.** Backup retention and restoration procedures have been documented and reviewed. |
| **Q4: Prepare for future growth** | Review and plan the next investment | Review reporting reliability, replica performance and operating costs. Assess whether the current arrangement can support Nigeria's expected orders, inventory and reporting requirements. Review whether the existing access and retention controls remain suitable. Consider forecasting or more frequent reporting only where a specific business need has been identified. | **1.** Management receives a review of reporting reliability, replica performance and the costs of maintaining the current arrangement. **2.** Management has an assessment of whether the current arrangement can support Nigeria's expected orders, inventory and reporting requirements. **3.** Any proposed future investment, such as forecasting, a warehouse or more frequent reporting, is supported by an identified business need and a clear reason for the additional cost. |

## Build Order and Business Outcomes

The order is deliberate.

- **Q1 protects the business:** Kloud Kart addresses the external compliance deadline and separates reporting activity from the live website.
- **Q2 establishes consistent figures:** Nana and Finance agree on definitions and receive the same reports.
- **Q3 improves reliability:** The company checks whether reports are complete and whether customer-data controls work in practice.
- **Q4 supports informed investment:** Management reviews the evidence before committing to more advanced technology or expansion-related changes.

The roadmap is measured through business outcomes rather than technology milestones alone. Provisioning a replica is not sufficient by itself; reporting must move safely, website performance must be measured and management must receive reliable figures.
